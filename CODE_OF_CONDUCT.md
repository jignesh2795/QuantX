# Contributor Code of Conduct

QuantX is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone who participates in its development and community.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the QuantX community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility, apologizing to those affected, and learning from mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment, intimidation, or threats.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or other community spaces.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code applies within project spaces and when an individual is officially representing QuantX in public spaces. Examples of representation include using an official project account, posting as a project representative, or acting as an appointed maintainer.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code fairly and consistently. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when doing so is safe and appropriate.

## Reporting

Do not report a sensitive conduct concern in a public issue. Contact the repository owner or maintainers privately through a trusted private communication route that you have independently established with them.

The repository does not currently publish a dedicated conduct-reporting email address or verified private contact route. A public contact route will not be invented in this policy. Maintainers should publish a reliable confidential reporting route before expanding community participation.
